Specialty Areas

Conditions Treated

Structured, documented Siddha support protocols delivered in coordination with your primary conventional medical treatments.

Cancer supportive therapy

புற்றுநோய் துணை சிகிச்சை

Symptom relief and strength-building during and after chemotherapy or radiotherapy, coordinated with your oncologist.

Common complaints

Chemo fatigue & nausea Mouth ulcers Low appetite Poor immunity

Our approach

  • Symptom-specific herbal preparations
  • Digestive and immunity restoration
  • Post-treatment recovery and follow-up protocols

Male infertility

ஆண் மலட்டுத்தன்மை

Structured Siddha management of low sperm count, poor motility and reproductive vitality concerns.

Common complaints

Oligospermia Low motility Erectile & libido concerns Hormonal imbalance

Our approach

  • Kayakalpa and vaajikarana medicines
  • Lifestyle, sleep and metabolic correction
  • Semen analysis at baseline and every cycle
Consult for this

PLHIV / AIDS supportive care

எச்.ஐ.வி பராமரிப்பு

Siddha adjuvant therapy for people living with HIV, always alongside antiretroviral treatment — never as a replacement.

Common complaints

Recurrent infections Weight & appetite loss Chronic fatigue Skin and GI complaints

Our approach

  • Deva Chooranam and allied internal medicines
  • Immunity and nutrition-focused diet plan
  • Regular CD4 and viral-load review with your ART centre
Consult for this

Important

Siddha alongside, never instead of

Patients are encouraged to continue their prescribed antiretroviral, oncology or fertility treatment. Siddha medicine here is supportive care delivered with your treating doctor’s knowledge.
