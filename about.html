Biography

Classical wisdom, clinical discipline

Dr. Thangadurai's work sits at the meeting point of Tamil Siddha tradition and contemporary evidence — treating chronic, stigmatised and difficult conditions with dignity, documentation and follow-up.

  1. Academic foundation

    Postgraduate training in Siddha medicine (MD(S)) with further qualifications in ACP(EM) and DCACR & HM, specialising in Pothu Maruthuvam (General Medicine).

  2. National Institute of Siddha, Chennai

    Served as Associate Professor (Contract) in the Pothu Maruthuvam Department at NIS, Tambaram Sanatorium — teaching postgraduates and running specialty OPD clinics.

  3. Research leadership

    Led and guided studies on Siddha interventions for cancer supportive care, male infertility, and PLHIV/AIDS (notably Deva Chooranam).

  4. Principal, JSA Medical College

    Heads JSA Medical College for Siddha and Research Centre, shaping curriculum, clinical standards and research culture for the next generation.

  5. Author & educator

    Published books on Siddha MCQs, management of male infertility and care of people living with PLHIV/AIDS, alongside public awareness content.
